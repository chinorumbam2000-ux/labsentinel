## Design in one paragraph

The Composite Outbreak Signal Score, EWMA and CUSUM ran unchanged, with their application defaults,
on 15 synthetic scenarios with known ground truth, 100 seeded runs each
(seed 20260930). Every outbreak starts on 2024-04-19. Pooled over the 13 primary scenarios (1300 realizations).
Detection: Composite at High or Critical; EWMA at STATISTICAL ALERT; CUSUM when either metric reaches h.

## Tradeoffs (no method is declared best)

- **Composite** — more conservative: the lowest false-alert burden (0.82 per 100 normal days). Slower and less sensitive in some scenarios: detected 58/100 slow gradual and 37/100 single-facility outbreaks.
- **EWMA** — more sensitive (900/900 outbreak runs) and earlier in some gradual scenarios (slow gradual median 8 vs 11 days). Higher false-alert burden (7.63 per 100 normal days), including 100/100 volume-only surges.
- **CUSUM** — sensitivity similar to EWMA (900/900 outbreak runs, median delay 5 days), responsive to sustained shifts; the highest false-alert burden here (10.55 per 100 normal days).

## What this does and does not show

- It shows how the three prototype methods behave, relative to each other, on designed synthetic data.
- It does not establish clinical or epidemiological validity, real-world PPV, or performance on real outbreaks.
- Weaknesses it found are documented as future work, not tuned away: see `/architecture` and
  [backend/README.md](../backend/README.md#weaknesses-found-recommendations-not-redesigns).
